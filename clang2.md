# Clang

## Players

* 2–5 players.
* Use a standard 52-card deck.
* Before each round, agree on a stake amount **X** and a eat amount *Y*

## Card Values

* Ace = 1
* 2–10 = Face value
* J, Q, K = 10

## Setup

Deal **5 cards** to each player.

If any player starts with **exactly 21 points**, they may immediately shout **"Clang!"**. The round ends and every other player pays them **X**.

Otherwise, play begins.

---

## Turn

On your turn, choose one of the following:

### 1. Play

* Discard **all cards of one rank** from your hand.
* Before you draw, the next player may choose to **Eat** (see below).
* Draw **1 card**.
* Turn ends.

### 2. Call Clang

Instead of playing, shout **"Clang!"**

* All players reveal their hands.
* The player with the **lowest total hand value** wins.
* If you are the lowest, every other player pays you **X**.
* Otherwise, you pay the lowest player **X × (number of other players)**.
* The round ends.

---

## Eat

Immediately after a player discards cards, **before they draw**, only the **next player in turn order** may **Eat** if they hold at least one card of the same rank.

When a player Eats:

1. The discarding player immediately pays them **X**.
2. The eating player discards **all cards of that rank** from their own hand.
3. The discarding player still draws **1 card** to finish their turn.
4. The eating player does **not** draw a card.
5. The eating player's normal turn is skipped.
6. Play continues with the following player.

Example:

* Turn order: A → B → C.
* A discards a 9.
* B Eats by discarding all of their 9s.
* A pays B **X** and then draws 1 card.
* B's turn is skipped.
* C takes the next turn.

---

## End of Round

A round ends when:

* A player starts with exactly **21** and calls **Clang**, or
* A player calls **Clang** during their turn.

Settle all payments, shuffle, and begin the next round.
